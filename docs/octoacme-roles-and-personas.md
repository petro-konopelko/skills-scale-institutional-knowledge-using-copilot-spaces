# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Lead (or Technical Lead) owns the technical direction and engineering execution for a project or product area. They bridge the gap between business requirements and implementation, ensuring that the team delivers high-quality, scalable solutions aligned with architectural standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate the technical architecture and design decisions
- Review and approve significant code changes and technical approaches
- Identify and manage technical risks, debt, and dependencies
- Mentor developers and promote engineering best practices
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feasibility, effort estimates, and trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on technical timelines and milestone planning

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound solutions that are maintainable and scalable
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned rework
- Build team capability and engineering culture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Engineering syncs and one-on-ones with developers
- Joint planning sessions with Product Managers and Project Managers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides technical guidance, reviews designs, unblocks implementation challenges
- **Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and scope trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Shares technical dependency maps and highlights risks affecting delivery timelines

---

## Quality / Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Quality / Release Manager ensures that releases meet the required quality bar before reaching production. They own the test strategy, release readiness process, and coordinate validation activities across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the overall test strategy and quality standards
- Own release checklists, go/no-go criteria, and smoke test plans
- Coordinate testing activities across developers, QA engineers, and stakeholders
- Track defect resolution and release blockers
- Manage release schedules and production validation processes
- Ensure compliance with regulatory or operational requirements where applicable

### Goals
- Prevent regressions and defects from reaching end users
- Increase release confidence and deployment frequency
- Create repeatable, efficient release processes

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reports and go/no-go meeting facilitation
- Defect triage sessions and bug prioritization with developers
- Post-release retrospectives and quality metrics reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinates on bug resolution, test coverage, and release branching
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on acceptance criteria and validates that features meet quality expectations
- **Project Managers**: Reports release status, blockers, and schedule impact

---

## Delivery Operations / Program Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Delivery Operations / Program Coordinator supports the planning cadence and operational efficiency of project delivery. They track cross-team dependencies, maintain reporting cadences, and ensure logistics and communications run smoothly across the program.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain program-level dependency tracking and delivery dashboards
- Coordinate planning ceremonies, status cadences, and cross-team sync meetings
- Consolidate and distribute status reports to stakeholders
- Track action items, decisions, and follow-ups across teams
- Identify and escalate delivery risks and blockers to the appropriate owners
- Support resource planning and capacity forecasting

### Goals
- Improve visibility and predictability across the delivery program
- Reduce coordination overhead for project teams
- Ensure consistent reporting and stakeholder alignment

### Typical Communication
- Weekly program status reports and dependency maps
- Cross-team coordination meetings and stakeholder briefings
- Action item logs and decision registers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Acts as a force multiplier, handling operational details so PMs can focus on risk and stakeholder management
- **Product Managers**: Aggregates roadmap dependencies and surfaces timeline conflicts
- **Developers**: Coordinates logistics for planning events and tracks team-level commitments

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
The Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder provides strategic oversight, organizational support, and funding authority for the project. They champion the initiative at the leadership level, remove organizational blockers, and make final calls on major trade-offs and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Set and communicate the strategic vision and success criteria for the initiative
- Secure and maintain budget, resources, and executive support
- Resolve escalations and cross-organizational conflicts that cannot be handled at the team level
- Review and approve major scope, schedule, or investment changes
- Hold accountability for overall project outcomes and business impact

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value and strategic outcomes
- Maintain executive and organizational alignment throughout delivery
- Protect the team from organizational obstacles and priority conflicts

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering committee meetings and milestone reviews
- Escalation decisions and approval of major change requests
- High-level status briefs and business outcome reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receives escalations and provides decisions on scope and priority conflicts
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on product vision and strategic priorities
- **Engineering Lead**: Reviews significant architectural or technical investment decisions

---

## Customer / UX Advocate

### Role Summary
The Customer / UX Advocate represents the voice of the end user throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure that product decisions are grounded in real user needs and that the experience delivered meets usability and accessibility standards.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct and synthesize user research, usability studies, and feedback sessions
- Define user personas, journey maps, and usability acceptance criteria
- Review designs and prototypes for alignment with user needs and accessibility standards
- Advocate for user-centered design decisions in prioritization and trade-off discussions
- Collect and analyze post-release user feedback and share insights with the team

### Goals
- Ensure the delivered product meets real user needs and expectations
- Reduce friction and improve user satisfaction and adoption
- Embed user empathy into the team's decision-making process

### Typical Communication
- User research readouts and design review sessions
- Usability feedback reports and UX acceptance criteria
- Post-release feedback summaries shared with Product Managers and developers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Provides user insights that inform prioritization, acceptance criteria, and product strategy
- **Developers**: Shares usability requirements, interaction specifications, and accessibility needs
- **Project Managers**: Flags UX-related risks that could affect release quality or adoption
