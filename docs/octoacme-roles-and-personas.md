# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads provide technical direction, enforce engineering quality, and help the team turn product goals into executable delivery plans. They bridge product intent with engineering feasibility and technical execution.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture direction, and key design standards
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and trade-offs
- Break down complex work into engineering increments and guide execution priorities
- Identify and mitigate technical risks, dependencies, and delivery bottlenecks
- Support developers through design reviews, technical decision-making, and escalation paths

### Goals
- Maintain a sustainable and scalable technical architecture
- Improve delivery predictability and engineering quality
- Keep cross-functional work aligned with product outcomes and constraints

### Typical Communication
- Architecture and design reviews
- Technical risk and dependency updates in planning and weekly syncs
- Escalation of blockers that affect delivery, quality, or scope

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate outcomes into feasible implementation plans
- Coordinates with Project Managers on sequencing, staffing, and milestone risks
- Guides Developers to maintain standards, reduce rework, and improve delivery quality
- Supports QA and Release stakeholders on testability, rollout readiness, and operational risk

---

## Quality / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Quality and Release Managers ensure that work is ready to ship, testable, and validated against expected outcomes. They focus on release quality, acceptance checks, and operational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy, regression coverage, and release quality checks
- Validate that acceptance criteria are met before release readiness decisions
- Coordinate smoke tests, rollout verification, and post-release follow-up
- Track release blockers, known issues, and rollback readiness
- Partner with engineering and stakeholders on production confidence and operational readiness

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and avoid preventable production issues
- Improve confidence in release quality and user experience
- Create a repeatable quality and release process across projects

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and sign-off checkpoints
- QA and smoke test reporting during sprint and release milestones
- Incident and rollback status updates when risks surface

### Interaction with existing roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and feature readiness
- Works with Project Managers on release timing, milestones, and communication plans
- Collaborates with Developers to verify fixes, regression coverage, and testability
- Coordinates with stakeholders and leadership on readiness decisions and escalation when quality gaps are identified

---

## Delivery Operations / Program Coordinator

### Role Summary
Delivery Operations or Program Coordinators support project rhythm, coordination, and execution logistics. They help keep meetings, dependencies, documentation, and reporting consistent across the team.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain planning cadence, meeting schedules, and action tracking
- Support dependency mapping, risk tracking, and status reporting
- Coordinate communication across multiple workstreams or teams
- Help capture decisions, follow-ups, and action owners from project meetings
- Keep process artifacts current so stakeholders have a single source of truth

### Goals
- Improve team coordination and reduce communication gaps
- Ensure execution stays on track through clear documentation and follow-up
- Support predictable project operations for cross-functional work

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status summaries and meeting notes
- Dependency and risk tracking updates
- Cross-team alignment check-ins and escalations

### Interaction with existing roles
- Supports Project Managers by maintaining schedules, reporting, and documentation
- Helps Product Managers track roadmap milestones and stakeholder updates
- Coordinates with Developers, QA, and Leads to monitor blockers and follow-through
- Provides status visibility to stakeholders without replacing decision ownership by PM or product leadership

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide strategic support, funding alignment, and executive sponsorship for the project. They help ensure the initiative remains aligned with business priorities and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic value, sponsorship, and business priority of the initiative
- Support funding, staffing, and cross-functional prioritization decisions
- Help resolve escalated conflicts or organizational blockers
- Review major progress milestones, risk levels, and investment trade-offs
- Champion the project across broader stakeholder groups and leadership forums

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers measurable business value
- Protect strategic alignment and executive visibility
- Enable timely decisions when trade-offs or escalations arise

### Typical Communication
- Executive updates and milestone reviews
- Steering committee briefings and decision moments
- Escalation summaries when business impact or resourcing is at stake

### Interaction with existing roles
- Provides direction to Product Managers and Project Managers on priority and business context
- Supports Engineering Leads and delivery teams when resources, scope, or cross-team dependencies need executive intervention
- Works with stakeholders to maintain alignment on business outcomes and strategic trade-offs

---

## Customer / UX Advocate

### Role Summary
Customer or UX Advocates ensure product decisions are grounded in user needs, experience quality, and service outcomes. They bring the voice of the customer into delivery discussions and help validate usability.

### Responsibilities
- Represent user needs, experience goals, and accessibility considerations
- Review solutions against usability, quality, and customer impact
- Help prioritize features based on customer value and usage pain points
- Support validation of outcomes and identify opportunities for improvement after launch
- Contribute to acceptance criteria that reflect real user scenarios

### Goals
- Improve customer value and product usability
- Ensure user experience is considered alongside technical and business constraints
- Strengthen the connection between product delivery and outcome quality

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and feedback summaries
- UX validation and usability review sessions
- Product and release feedback loops

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to refine problem statements and success metrics
- Works with Developers and Engineering Leads to review usability and implementation trade-offs
- Helps Project Managers and QA teams assess whether the delivered experience meets customer expectations and acceptance criteria

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify ownership, decision rights, and communication patterns across the project lifecycle.
